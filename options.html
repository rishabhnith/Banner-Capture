Welcome! Three steps to your first capture

  1. Add a website below (Boulanger, Fnac and Darty are already there).
  2. Pick its banner: click Pick banner, then click the banner on the page. Choose the whole carousel so every slide is captured.
  3. Capture: use the toolbar icon or the button at the top. Files go to your Downloads folder.
If Chrome asks where to save every file, turn off “Ask where to save each file before downloading”.
